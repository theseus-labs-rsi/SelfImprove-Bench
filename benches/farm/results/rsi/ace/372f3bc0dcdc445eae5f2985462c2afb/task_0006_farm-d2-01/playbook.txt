Exclude crops that cannot mature in time because they cannot contribute to realized profit.

Track each day’s cash, free plots, planted batches with their harvest dates, and harvested inventory; after each day’s buy, plant, harvest, and sell actions, use released plots and sale proceeds to evaluate subsequent plantings.

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

[err-00004] helpful=3 harmful=0 :: Do not treat maturity_days as an inclusive duration: under the stated rule, harvest_day = plant_day + maturity_days. Also avoid buying or planting more seeds than current cash or free farmland allows, and remember that only harvested and sold fruit affects final cash and profit.
[err-00009] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Never use same-day harvest or sale proceeds to fund purchases on that day: validate every buy against the cash balance at the start of the buy phase, then update cash only after the later harvest and sell phases. Recalculate each batch cost as seed_price × quantity and verify it against that pre-purchase balance.
## PROBLEM-SOLVING HEURISTICS

[ph-00003] helpful=0 harmful=1 :: When all feasible crops have positive per-plant profit and only one harvest is possible within the horizon, prioritize the crop with the best feasible total profit after accounting for seed cost, yield, sale price, cash, and plot limits. Do not spend money on crops whose harvest falls outside the task duration.
[ph-00008] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not infer global optimality from the highest per-plant margin or from the best initial crop. Enumerate feasible daily transitions, or use dynamic programming over cash, free land, and outstanding batches; explicitly test harvest-day reinvestment because a crop harvested early can fund and enable another round before the deadline.
